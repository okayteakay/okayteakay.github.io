I'm a Machine Learning Engineer at [Kensho Technologies](https://kensho.com) (a subsidiary of S&P Global), where I build unstructured data retrieval agents and their evaluation frameworks over S&P's rich financial datasets — accelerating AI-driven research for analysts and investment professionals. I'm also researching LLM judge calibration for document text quality rating, using item response theory to align LLM jury ratings with human judgments. Previously on Kensho's Document Intelligence team, I productionized ML models for document understanding, improved figure and chart parsing in production pipelines, and co-authored a large-scale table extraction dataset submitted to ECCV 2026.

I hold an Masters in in Data Science from [NYU's Center for Data Science](https://cds.nyu.edu/) and a Bachelors of Technoogy degree in Electrical Engineering from AMU, India.

Beyond my professional work, I'm passionate about making STEM accessible to the next generation. I served as a research mentor and teaching assistant for [NYU's GSTEM program](https://wp.nyu.edu/gstem/) (2024–2025), guiding high school girls through hands-on physics & data science research projects.
